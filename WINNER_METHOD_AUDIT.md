# Winner method audit — E180, 2026-11-16

The current target is **75% exact winner accuracy across all races**, predicted before the weekend. Strict podium overlap remains secondary. The highest observed development candidate is E179: **34/70 = 48.6%** winners, versus standings and frozen `ens` at **32/70 = 45.7%**. Its race-paired difference against `ens` is +2.86 percentage points, 95% interval −4.29 to +10.00; inner accuracy loses one race to the winner baseline. This is no demonstrated improvement, and the target remains unmet.

This audit reads research methods, not outside text as forecast features. No new model score, source extraction, market input or 2026 evaluation was produced. The three papers below do not establish 75% exact-winner forecasting under our cutoff. This limited audit does not prove that the target is impossible.

## Prioritized follow-up avenues

**Status after E181:** the fixed online Laplace joint filter failed its inner gate: 30/61 exact winners versus E168 32/61, equal winning-team hits and worse winner log-loss. No outer scoring. Do not tune that formulation after these outcomes. E182 verified raw-name history splits in the main feature path; the existing E139 lineage map has not been applied there. A fixed inner-only winner comparison is the next test; the broader hypotheses below remain unproven.

1. **Joint causal driver/constructor inference from completed qualifying and race outcomes.** E80 used a two-layer Gaussian pace Kalman filter; E99 fused noisy team indicators; E30/E169 fitted rank/choice likelihoods on features. These do not jointly filter continuous qualifying observations and non-Gaussian race rankings through shared evolving states. A bounded inner-only prototype could test that specific distinction. Use only completed earlier events, freeze the state before the target weekend, and handle retirees explicitly rather than silently remove them from evaluation. Remove target grid from the forecast equation or integrate a genuinely prior forecast over it. Full-sample smoothing and globally fitted observation scales are forbidden. This supplies a different assumption, not new information or evidence that 75% is reachable.

   Before implementation, register one formulation, fixed computation budget and inner decision rule. Independently mutate target/future qualifying, rankings and retirements; predictions must remain unchanged, while earlier-source changes must affect them. Compare full-field exact winners and winning-team accuracy with standings, `ens` and E168 on the same 61 inner races; report probability quality too. Do not proceed to outer unless the pre-registered inner gate passes. Do not install a heavyweight sampler merely to reproduce an explanatory paper.

2. **Constructor continuity audit, low priority.** Check existing canonical team identities and whether a historical rebrand resets useful observations. Fix only a demonstrated mapping error using structured existing identifiers. Most leading teams in the evaluated period retain identity, so this has no established path to the required gain. Do not introduce subjective ownership/upgrade narratives or tune lineage choices against outer outcomes.

3. **Winner-relevant pace measurement before more telemetry modelling.** E174's hindsight median clean-lap pace rule gets only 39/70 exact winners, even with the target race's pace. That particular proxy is weak for winner selection; it is not a bound on every pace measure. Any richer historical race-trim profile needs a pre-registered inner measurement/transfer test before another winner classifier. Existing negative telemetry and tyre-management experiments remain closed unless the proposed measurement is materially different and independently verifiable.

No candidate is promoted by this audit. Keep the denominator, pre-weekend guard, shuffled-winner comparator and paired baseline comparisons. Outer 2023–25 has been inspected repeatedly and is development evidence; 2026 remains restricted and is not pristine. The initial broad methods search exposed incidental 2026 result snippets, logged in E180, without opening those pages or using them for modelling. Subsequent inspection used the specific primary research pages above.
